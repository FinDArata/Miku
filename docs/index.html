Dictation, notes and your voice

Kanon now has its own window, on Windows and Linux, with everything it does in one place.

  • Flow dictation: hold a key anywhere, speak, let go: your words are pasted where you type. Two taps for hands-free, a dictionary, snippets, a style per app and your statistics.
  • Parakeet, on your computer: NVIDIA’s speech recognition runs offline, in 25 languages. Downloaded once, only when you click Download.
  • Notetaker: record a call (no bot joins it) and get the transcript person by person, with a summary and the to-dos. The audio is deleted once the transcript is written.
  • « OK Kanon »: say it and ask anything: add a pill, play music, your Stripe sales, the weather, an email to prepare. Claude answers with your own key; your voice never leaves your computer.
Private by design. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Your keys stay in Windows Credential Manager or the Linux Secret Service. Read the privacy policy.

Windows 10/11: the button downloads the .msi installer; prefer an .exe? Here it is. Not signed yet, so SmartScreen may ask: click More info → Run anyway. Linux: version 0.5 is out (AppImage, .deb, .rpm), download it here.
